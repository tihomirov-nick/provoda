PROVODA
=======

INSTALLING
Open Provoda right from this window. It moves itself to the Applications
folder and restarts from there. Without administrator rights, Provoda goes
to the Applications folder inside your home folder. Once it runs, its icon
appears in the menu bar at the top right of the screen.

You can also install Provoda by hand. Drag its icon onto the Applications
folder next to it and launch Provoda from the Applications folder.

IF macOS WON'T OPEN THE APP
The app isn't notarized by Apple, so on the first launch macOS may show
a warning ("cannot verify the developer" or "Apple could not verify it is free
of malware"). You only need to allow it once.

  • On macOS 15 and later, close the warning with "Done", open System
    Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
    next to Provoda and confirm.

  • On macOS 14, right-click Provoda in Finder, choose "Open", then click
    "Open" again in the warning.

  • If that didn't help, drag Provoda onto the Applications folder, open
    Terminal and run:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Provoda.app

THE MENU BAR ICON
A click on the icon opens Provoda's panel. A right click or a Control-click
opens the menu: settings, checking for updates, About and Quit.

WHAT IT SHOWS
Provoda's panel lists what is connected over USB and Thunderbolt: the name,
the protocol and speed, the power when macOS reports it, the maker and the
type. The list updates by itself. The search field at the bottom looks
through names, makers, types and speeds. In Settings you can show serial numbers
and hubs and turn on a notification for every new device.

LANGUAGE
Provoda works in Russian and English. In Settings (⌘,) choose "Same as
System", "Русский" or "English". With "Same as System" Provoda takes the
first suitable language from the list in System Settings > General >
Language & Region. A new language takes effect after a restart, and the
"Restart" button appears right under the choice.

UPDATES
Provoda looks for a new version on GitHub at launch, every three hours and
after the Mac wakes up. It downloads a new version by itself, checks its
signature and installs it when Provoda is idle. With "Install updates
automatically" turned off in Settings, the icon gets a dot instead, and
Provoda's panel shows a card with "Later" and "Update".

OPEN AT LOGIN
Provoda opens at login to install updates on time. To stop it, turn off
"Open at login" in Settings.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 14 or later, a Mac with Apple Silicon or Intel.
